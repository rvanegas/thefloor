1. SCREEN RECORDING AND DEVICES
None attached; one of an earlier release would misrepresent this. Section 3
walks it in order; we will record any part on request. No purchases, no
tracking, no ATT prompt. Tested on iPhone 12 Pro Max, 13 mini, 16e, 16 Pro and
16 Pro Max, iOS 18.5 and 26.6, physical and portrait only; iPad in iPhone
compatibility mode only.

2. WHAT IT DOES
Voice conversation between people who already know each other. A channel holds
up to six and persists between conversations; anyone present may claim "the
floor", withholding every other microphone for a minute. New: watch parties
(6), transcripts (4), profiles, one account on several devices.

3. GETTING IN AND REACHING FEATURES
No passwords; a six-digit code is normally emailed. This account has a fixed

You do not need a second person. The account holds one contact, Sam Rivera, and

Open "Weekly Convo" > Step in (microphone prompt). Record/Stop - nothing asks
for a name; Rename, Play, Export, Transcribe and Delete are on the recording's
row. "Play something together" plays a file from the phone into the room, and
"Watch something together" takes a YouTube link - section 6. "Shared clipboard"
holds one text item and "Share a guest link" is at the foot of that screen; the
channel's Settings revokes links and holds Notifications. On Home, Contacts and
Settings are the top buttons and "Start a channel" the last row; a contact's
profile lists the channels you share. Settings holds Appearance, Sign out other
devices, Privacy policy and Delete account.

NOT BUGS: "Claim the floor" is unavailable while alone, there being no room to
ask for quiet; a just-stopped recording greys Play and Export while its mix is
being made.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
LiveKit (open-source, self-hosted by us) for audio and recordings; S3 to store
them; Amazon SES for sign-in codes; APNs; Ko-fi for donations; AssemblyAI, sent
only a recording somebody asked to have transcribed. No third-party sign-in,
payment processor or advertising. Transcribing is never automatic - a button on
one recording, behind a confirmation - and free once per account, then disabled
with the reason under it.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The Support screen's Ko-fi link appears only in the US storefront, per
3.1.1(a). Voluntary, unlocks nothing; the app is free everywhere.

6. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL, AND WHAT A WATCH PARTY IS
All content is user-created; a member may play their own audio file into a
channel, and any member can delete any recording. A watch party carries no
video: this app never fetches, decodes, stores or shows a frame, it shares a
position and a clock. The phone is the remote; "Watch on another screen" mints
a link to a web page opened on a laptop or tablet, where YouTube's own IFrame
player plays it unmodified, unobscured and with its own controls. Nothing is
extracted, and a party running refuses to record.

GUIDELINE 1.2: no discovery and no directory - nothing can be browsed and
nobody found. The one field taking an email address does not search: it sends a
request there and returns nothing, disclosing not even whether an account
exists. Contacts require acceptance on both sides, only an accepted contact can
be added to a channel, and an address appears on a profile only if that person
chose to show it.

Guest links are the one way someone without an account takes part. They are an
invitation, not an open door: opening one joins nothing, the visitor types a
name and knocks, and a member must admit them. Any member present can withdraw
a guest's microphone or remove them, revoking their link; links are also
revoked from Settings, and every guest goes when the last member steps out.
Recording is never automatic and its indicator shows to all, guests included.
