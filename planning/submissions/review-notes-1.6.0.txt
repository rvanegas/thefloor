1. SCREEN RECORDING AND DEVICES
None attached; section 3 walks it. No purchases, no tracking, no ATT prompt.
iPhone is portrait and turns sideways only for a film; iPad takes all four.

2. WHAT IT DOES
Voice conversation between people who know each other. A channel holds up to
six and outlasts a conversation; anyone present may claim "the floor",
withholding every other microphone for a minute. New here: a watch party's
film plays in the app (section 6), a lock screen card, and swipes between
channel and Home.

3. GETTING IN AND REACHING FEATURES
No passwords; a code is emailed. This account has a fixed
one: appreview@rvanegas.co / SIX-DIGIT CODE GOES HERE BEFORE SUBMITTING

It holds a contact, Sam Rivera, and three channels; "Weekly Convo" has
recordings.

Home switches between Channels, Contacts and Support. Open "Weekly Convo".
The bottom bar is Mute, Claim and the rungs In, Nearby and Out; stepping in
prompts for the microphone. Tapping a member opens their profile: "Mute
them", Ping.

The tabs across the top: Notepad, Invite (a contact, or "Share a guest
link"), Listen (an audio file from this phone), Recordings and Watch - copy
a YouTube link, then "Watch something together" (section 6). "Record
automatically" is in the channel's Settings. Floor Settings, from Home,
carries Getting started, "Choose where sound comes out", Email and Labs,
which reveals Transcribe on a recording's row.

NOT BUGS: a channel holds you on one device at a time; "Claim the floor" is
unavailable while alone; Ping is offered only to somebody in the channel or
nearby it; a recording starts one run per sitting, so Stop is final until
the room empties; "Mute them" goes once that person is muted, and only they
can reopen it; the room is muted, with no button to lift it, while a film
plays on the device you stepped in on.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
LiveKit (open-source, self-hosted) for audio and recordings; S3 stores them;
SES for sign-in codes; APNs; Ko-fi; YouTube's IFrame player, only once a
member starts a watch party; AssemblyAI, sent only a recording somebody asked
to transcribe, with Labs on. No third-party sign-in, payment processor or
advertising.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The Ko-fi link on Support shows only in the US storefront (3.1.1(a));
voluntary, unlocks nothing, and the app is free.

6. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL AND WATCH PARTIES
A member may play their own audio file in; any member may delete a
recording. A watch party carries no video of ours: this app never fetches,
decodes, stores or republishes a frame; what travels through it is a
position and a clock. YouTube's own IFrame player plays the video, embedded
unmodified, unobscured and with its own controls, in a web view on the
member's own device or a second device of the same account. Nothing is
downloaded or separated from its audio, and a channel with a party loaded
will not record.

GUIDELINE 1.2: no discovery and no directory - nothing can be browsed,
nobody found. A username names an invite link, not a search term, minted by
its owner and seating one person. The field taking an email address does not
search: a request goes there and nothing comes back, not even whether an
account exists. Contacts need both sides to accept, only a contact may be
added to a channel, an address shows only if that person chose to, and Ping
is contacts-only.

The one place strangers meet: signing up without an invitation and knowing
nobody puts you, once, into "Getting Started Cohort" with up to three others
who joined then and one of us - nobody searched, nobody becomes a contact,
only display names show, and leaving is a tap. This account is not in one.

Guests: a visitor with a link knocks under a typed name and a member admits
them. Any member can withdraw a guest's microphone or remove them, revoking
the link; every guest goes when the last member steps out. Recording happens
only by a tap or the channel's setting, and its indicator shows to guests.
