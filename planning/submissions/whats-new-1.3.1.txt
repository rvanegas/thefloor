The Floor now runs in a web browser. Go to thefloor.rvanegas.co and sign in with the same account you use on your phone. You get your channels, the floor, and your recordings. Notifications still go to your phone; a browser tab can only show a dot on itself. You can only be in a channel on one device at a time. If you open a channel in the browser while you are already in it on your phone, the browser now shows a Step In button rather than acting as though you are already there.

Guests can now be added as contacts. If someone joins a channel through a guest link, any member of that channel can ask them to become a contact. The guest answers on the guest page, without leaving the conversation, and creates an account there if they do not have one. Each member's request is answered separately, so declining one does not decline the others. If you open a guest link for a channel you already belong to, you join as yourself instead of as a guest.

The Contacts screen and profiles have a new layout. Add contact is now the first row. Your own card is below it, and tapping it opens your profile; editing your name and bio is now behind an Edit button on that profile rather than on a separate screen. A profile shows availability first, then the ways to contact the person: Ping appears at the top when you opened the profile from a channel they are not in, followed by their email address. Your own profile now shows the email address you are signed in with.

The Home screen now counts other people. The time under each channel is when someone other than you was last in it, and channels are sorted by that. Channels someone else has visited sort above channels only you have opened. The mark showing that you stepped in and out now lasts fifteen minutes instead of five, matching how long other members' rosters still list you as nearby.

The floor is released when the person holding it disconnects. Previously the room stayed silenced for up to a minute waiting for a speaker who had already dropped. Disconnections are also detected faster: five to seven seconds instead of seven to seventeen. The roster now shows when someone is still present but no longer sending audio, before their connection times out.

Settings has a new Headphones option. Bluetooth headphones sound better when nobody is speaking and switch to a lower-quality connection when someone starts, which can clip the first word. Turn "Keep the connection steady" on and they stay on the speaking connection the whole time you are in a channel. Turn it off and the sound improves whenever the channel is quiet.
