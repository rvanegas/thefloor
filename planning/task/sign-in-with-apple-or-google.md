# Sign in with Apple or Google

Rodrigo, on the call with Erta, 2026-10-02: single sign-on from Apple or Google
is his favourite way into any app, and *"maybe I could implement that"*. Every
step of signing up loses people, and this is the one step that can be made
shorter. The complication is the implicit contact: an invitation is matched to
the email address or phone number it was sent to, and an Apple sign-in can
hide the address behind a relay. Goes with
`authenticate-with-phone-numbers-and-sms.md` and
`collapse-the-three-invitations-into-one.md`.
