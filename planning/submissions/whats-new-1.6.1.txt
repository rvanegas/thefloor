The Floor speaks Spanish. Floor Settings has a Language card - Automatic, English, Español - and the choice follows your account to every device you sign in on.

A channel can have a public page. Public page is on the channel's Settings; the page carries the channel's name, its notepad and the conversations published on it, and a feed podcast apps can subscribe to. No member is named on it, ever. Cover art, a category and a language are there for podcast directories.

A conversation goes onto that page only once everybody who was in it has agreed, one at a time, from "I agree this can be published" on the recording's card. Any one of them can take their agreement back, and that takes it down.

Home has a Podcasts tab, listing every channel that has a public page. Everybody in a channel is told, once, that it has one.

A contact can be asked into a channel as a guest rather than as a member: Guest and Member sit on each row of the Invite tab. A seat offered to somebody with an account opens in the app. A channel holds six members and forty guests, two of whom can hold the microphone.

Home says in words what is waiting for you, above the lists, and one tap answers it. Accepting a contact request opens the channel it makes.

A channel's first tab is called People.

Notice that somebody has stepped into a channel stops being sent to an account that has gone a week without opening the app, and starts again the moment it does. An invitation, a ping and an accepted contact go on arriving.

A channel remembers what it was watching, so the Watch tab can put the film back on without the link. A film that has run out can be started again, and a watch party survives the server restarting, coming back paused where it was.

The film moves between your devices while it is playing, and Home says when it is on another one, where a tap brings it here.
