# Contacts are one-on-one channels

Rodrigo's idea on the call with Erta, 2026-10-02, and he said *"I think maybe
I should do this"*: the contacts list **is** the list of one-on-one channels,
so none of them appears in the channels list, which then holds only group
channels. The reason: a contact has no other purpose than being someone to
talk to, so making the channel explicit is extra administration. It would
usually be private, but need not be. Erta liked it, though she was not yet
convinced that a contact should automatically carry a channel.

Becoming contacts already makes a pair channel (`backlog/a-standing-door-has-no-lock.md`
§ *And the pair channel*), so this is mostly about where it is shown and what
it is called — Rodrigo said he would rename it. It also bears on
`backlog/home-grows-without-limit-and-a-channel-can-only-be-left-from-inside-it.md`,
and is one of the two ways to collapse invitations in
`collapse-the-three-invitations-into-one.md`.
