1. SCREEN RECORDING AND DEVICES
None attached; section 3 walks it. No purchases, no tracking, no ATT
prompt. iPhone is portrait; iPad takes all four orientations.

2. WHAT IT DOES
Voice conversation between people who know each other. A channel holds up to
six and outlasts a conversation; anyone present may claim "the floor",
withholding every other microphone for a minute. New: Nearby, a middle state
holding no microphone and no audio, offering a step in when somebody arrives; a channel screen in tabs; a per-channel "Record automatically"; a
Support list on Home carrying Help, a question box; and a getting-started
checklist.

3. GETTING IN AND REACHING FEATURES
No passwords; a code is emailed. This account has a fixed
one: appreview@rvanegas.co / SIX-DIGIT CODE GOES HERE BEFORE SUBMITTING

The account holds a contact, Sam Rivera, and three channels; "Weekly Convo"
holds a recording, "Short Sample".

Home switches between Channels, Contacts and Support. Open "Weekly Convo".
The bar at the bottom is the controls: Mute, Claim, and the rungs In, Nearby
and Out. Stepping in prompts for the microphone. Tapping somebody in the
roster opens their profile, which carries "Mute them" above Ping.

The tabs across the top are the rest. Notepad is plain text behind an Edit,
with the shared clipboard above it. Invite holds the two ways in - a contact,
and "Share a guest link". Player plays an audio file from this phone.
Recordings holds Record/Stop, and Rename, Play, Share and Delete per row.
"Record automatically" is in the channel's Settings, in the header.

Settings > Labs > "Show experimental features" reveals Transcribe on a
recording's row and a Watch tab (section 6).

NOT BUGS: a channel holds you on one device at a time; "Claim the floor" is
unavailable while alone; Nearby lapses after fifteen minutes and the rung
restarts it; stepping into one channel leaves you Nearby in the others; a
channel recording automatically starts one run per sitting, so Stop is final
until the room empties; "Mute them" goes once that person is muted, only they
can open their microphone again.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
LiveKit (open-source, self-hosted by us) for audio and recordings; S3 stores
them; SES for sign-in codes; APNs; Ko-fi for donations; AssemblyAI, sent only
a recording somebody asked to have transcribed, reachable only with Labs on.
No third-party sign-in, payment processor or advertising.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The Support screen's Ko-fi link appears only in the US storefront (3.1.1(a)). Voluntary, unlocks nothing; the app is free.

6. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL AND WATCH PARTIES
A member may play their own audio file in; any member can delete any
recording. A watch party is behind Labs and carries no video: this app
never fetches, decodes, stores or shows a frame, it shares a position and a
clock. "Watch on another screen" mints a link to a page where YouTube's own
IFrame player plays it unmodified, unobscured, with its own controls.

GUIDELINE 1.2: no discovery and no directory - nothing can be browsed and
nobody found. A username names an invite link and is not a search term; new
accounts are given one derived from their address, editable. A link is minted
by its owner and seats one person. The field taking an email
address does not search: it sends a request there and returns nothing,
disclosing not even whether an account exists. Contacts need both sides to
accept, only a contact can be added to a channel, an address appears on a
profile only if that person chose to show it, and Ping is for contacts only.

Guest links are the one way someone without an account takes part: a visitor
knocks under a typed name and a member admits them. Any member can withdraw a
guest's microphone or remove them, revoking their link, and every guest goes
when the last member steps out. Recording happens only by a tap or by the
channel's setting, and its indicator shows to everybody, guests included.
