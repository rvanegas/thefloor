The Floor now runs in a web browser at thefloor.rvanegas.co, with your channels, the floor, and your recordings. Opening a channel there while you are already in it on your phone offers to step you in, and steps you out of the phone.

The channel screen has been rebuilt. Mute, the floor, and stepping in or out are now in a bar pinned to the bottom of the screen, reachable without scrolling, and the channel name, Home and Settings are pinned to the top — as they now are on Home and Contacts. The cards between them have been reordered, with the floor first, directly under the list of people. A new setting, "Repeat the channel controls as cards", turns the repeated cards off and leaves the bar.

Guests can be added as contacts. Anyone in a channel can ask someone who joined through a guest link to become a contact, and they answer on the guest page without leaving the conversation, creating an account there if they need one. Each member's request is answered separately. Opening a guest link for a channel you already belong to now takes you in as yourself.

Contacts and profiles have a new layout. Add contact is the first row, your own card is below it, and editing your name and bio is behind an Edit button on your profile. A profile leads with whether the person is about, then with the ways to reach them. Your own profile shows the email address you are signed in with, and you can change it: enter a new address and confirm it with the code sent there. The channels you share with someone are listed on their profile, tapping one takes you there, and the one you are in is marked. Ping is now offered for contacts only.

Declining an invitation leaves the channel, after asking you to confirm, on every device.

Home counts other people. The time under each channel is when someone other than you was last in it, and channels are sorted by that.

The floor is released as soon as the person holding it disconnects, and disconnections are detected in five to seven seconds. The roster shows when someone is present but no longer sending audio, and you can ping them in that state.

Appearance, tap to step in, and the new channel controls setting now follow your account, applying on every device you are signed in to and changing on all of them at once.

A new Headphones setting, "Keep the connection steady", holds Bluetooth headphones on the speaking connection for as long as you are in a channel, so nothing switches when somebody starts talking.
