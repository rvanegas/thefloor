1. SCREEN RECORDING AND DEVICES
None attached; section 3 walks it. No purchases, no tracking, no ATT prompt.
iPhone is portrait and turns sideways only for a film; iPad takes all four.

2. WHAT IT DOES
Voice conversation between people who know each other. A channel holds six
members and outlasts a conversation; anyone present may claim "the floor",
withholding every other microphone for a minute.

3. GETTING IN AND REACHING FEATURES
No passwords; a code is emailed. This account has a fixed
one: appreview@rvanegas.co / SIX-DIGIT CODE GOES HERE BEFORE SUBMITTING

It holds a contact, Sam Rivera, and three channels; "Weekly Convo" has
recordings.

Home switches between Channels, Contacts, Podcasts and Support. Open "Weekly
Convo". The bottom bar is Mute, Claim and the rungs In, Nearby and Out;
stepping in prompts for the microphone. Tapping a member opens their profile:
"Mute them", Ping.

The tabs across the top: People, Notepad, Invite (a contact as "Guest" or
"Member", or "Share a guest link"), Listen, Recordings and Watch - copy a
YouTube link, then "Watch something together". "Record automatically" and
"Public page" are in the channel's Settings. Floor Settings, from Home,
carries Language (Automatic, English, Espanol), Getting started, "Choose where
sound comes out", Email and Labs, which reveals Transcribe on a recording's
row.

NOT BUGS: a channel holds you on one device at a time; "Claim the floor" is
unavailable while alone; Ping is offered only to somebody in the channel or
nearby it; a recording starts one run per sitting, so Stop is final until the
room empties; the room is muted while a film plays on the device you stepped
in on; "Public page" is refused until the channel is named.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
LiveKit (open-source, self-hosted) for audio and recordings; S3 stores them and
cover art; SES for sign-in codes; APNs; Ko-fi; YouTube's IFrame player, only
once a member starts a watch party; AssemblyAI, sent only a recording somebody
asked to transcribe, with Labs on.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The Ko-fi link on Support shows only in the US storefront (3.1.1(a));
voluntary, unlocks nothing, and the app is free.

6. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL, PUBLISHING AND WATCH PARTIES
A member may play their own audio file in; any member may delete a recording.
A watch party carries no video of ours: what travels through it is a position
and a clock, and YouTube's own IFrame player plays the video in a web view,
unmodified, unobscured and with its own controls.

NEW: a channel may give itself a public web page and an RSS feed. A
conversation reaches it only when every account heard in it has agreed,
separately; any one of them withdrawing takes it down at once. No member is
named on it, ever, and every member is told the channel has a page. Both carry
a takedown address read by a person. The Podcasts tab frames that same list.

GUIDELINE 1.2: what can be found is channels that opted in, never people. A
username names an invite link, minted by its owner and seating one person. A
field taking an email address sends a request there and returns nothing.
Contacts need both sides to accept, only a contact may be added to a channel,
an address shows only if that person chose to, and Ping is contacts-only. The
one exception: signing up without an invitation and knowing nobody puts you,
once, into "Getting Started Cohort" with up to three others who joined then
and one of us - nobody searched, nobody becomes a contact, and leaving is a
tap. This account is not in one.

Guests: a visitor with a link knocks under a typed name and a member admits
them, or a contact is offered a seat, which opens in the app. Six members and
forty guests, two of whom may hold a microphone. Any member can withdraw a
guest's microphone or remove them; every guest goes when the last member steps
out. Recording happens only by a tap or the channel's setting, and its
indicator shows to guests.
