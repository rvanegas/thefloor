# Talk with Claude about an episode

Erta, 2026-11-16: a way for a listener to take part in a podcast's
conversation, better than leaving a comment — for instance opening a channel
from an episode and asking Claude *what were Rodrigo and Daniel saying here,
who disagreed with what*. Rodrigo thought it very good and had not thought of
it: it is engagement that needs nothing from the podcast's creators. No
concrete design yet. Questions to start from: whether a channel made from an
episode is an ordinary channel; how Claude is given the episode's transcript
(the channel library's document, or the published VTT); and how the episode's
chapters (`chapters-for-an-episode.md`) would let somebody start from a
moment.
