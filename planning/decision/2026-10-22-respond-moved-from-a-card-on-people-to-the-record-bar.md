# Respond moved from a card on People to the Record bar

Changed 2026-10-22, the day it was built, at Rodrigo's request: *remove the
card from People; design something comparable to the other controls on the
Record tab, with the same functionality; it should not look like the user
cards in People.* Build 386 shipped the card; the build after it does not.
What *Respond* does, and why, is
`2026-10-22-claude-joins-the-conversation-as-a-paste-in-the-record.md`, and
none of it changed — the server, the route, the rules, the *Response model*
and the meter are as they were. Only where the control is.

**The card read as a person.** It was a People card's shape with a person's
marks taken out — no dot, no clock, no profile — and what was left still said
*somebody is in this room*, which Claude is not. It is a control about the
*Record*: it reads the Record and writes into it, so it belongs with the
Record's controls, and the reply then lands in the log under it.

**A line of its own under the bar, not a glyph on the row.** The row — Play
and Stop, *Audio*, *Text*, the paste glyph — is about 328 of the 343 points a
375-point phone leaves, measured from a screenshot; a third glyph at 40 would
wrap. So it takes the shape *Several voices on my microphone* already has
under the row: the word, then its control, as every switch is. *Claude*, with
*· Pro* on a *Pro* channel, then *Respond*. Who asked, or why a press failed,
is the muted line under it, which is where the bar already puts its refusals.

**What went with the card**: its *Reads the Record and adds to it* line, its
*Step in to ask* — grey while away is what the switches do, with no sentence,
the footer already saying you are away — and the argument for renaming
*People*, which no longer holds a card that is not a person. `/privacy` says
*Record* tab rather than *People* tab; its date did not move, being the same
day.
