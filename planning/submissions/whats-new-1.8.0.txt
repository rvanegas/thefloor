A channel's members can remove one of their own, and it takes two of them. "Move to remove them" is at the foot of a member's profile; nothing happens until another member agrees, within a day. The person removed is told it happened and not who decided, and anybody can invite them back. A channel of two cannot remove anybody.

While you are in a room on one device, Home on your others pins it at the top: "On another device".

The Notepad tab is now Clipboard. What the notepad said is the channel's Description, in the channel's Settings under Public page, and it is shown only while the channel has a public page.

A chime sounds when a film starts and another when it stops, since the room's microphones close while it plays. Pausing a film gives everybody their microphone back at once.

People says "not watching" beside anybody in the room with the film on none of their devices.

A film resumes in step with the room on every device, and tapping the picture in the corner always opens it.

The floor can be claimed whenever no film is playing.

A guest made a member while in the room stays in the conversation, muted unless they were already speaking.

A bar on Home naming one invitation opens its channel, and one naming one contact request opens that person.

"In the app now" under a contact means they are using it, not only that it is open somewhere.
