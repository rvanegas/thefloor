1. SCREEN RECORDING AND DEVICES
None attached; section 3 walks it. No purchases, no tracking, no ATT prompt.
iPhone is portrait; iPad takes all four orientations.

2. WHAT IT DOES
Voice conversation between people who know each other. A channel holds up to
six and outlasts a conversation; anyone present may claim "the floor",
withholding every other microphone for a minute. New here: presence sounds,
recording alone, an Email setting, and the introductory channel in section 6.

3. GETTING IN AND REACHING FEATURES
No passwords; a code is emailed. This account has a fixed
one: appreview@rvanegas.co / SIX-DIGIT CODE GOES HERE BEFORE SUBMITTING

It holds a contact, Sam Rivera, and three channels; "Weekly Convo" holds
recordings.

Home switches between Channels, Contacts and Support. Open "Weekly Convo".
The bar at the bottom is the controls: Mute, Claim, and the rungs In, Nearby
and Out; stepping in prompts for the microphone. Tapping somebody in the
Members tab opens their profile, which carries "Mute them" above Ping.

The tabs across the top are the rest: Notepad (plain text behind an Edit,
shared clipboard above it), Invite (a contact, or "Share a guest link"),
Player (an audio file from this phone) and Recordings (Record/Stop, and
Rename, Play, Share and Delete per row). "Record automatically" is in the
channel's Settings. Floor Settings, from
Home, carries Getting started, Email and Labs; Labs > "Show experimental
features" reveals Transcribe on a recording's row and a Watch tab (section 6).

NOT BUGS: a channel holds you on one device at a time; "Claim the floor" is
unavailable while alone; Ping is offered only to somebody in the channel or
nearby it; a channel recording automatically starts one run per sitting, so Stop is final
until the room empties; "Mute them" goes once that person is muted; only they
can reopen their microphone.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
LiveKit (open-source, self-hosted) for audio and recordings; S3 stores
them; SES for sign-in codes; APNs; Ko-fi; AssemblyAI, sent only a recording
somebody asked to transcribe, reachable only with Labs on.
No third-party sign-in, payment processor or advertising.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The Ko-fi link on Support appears only in the US storefront (3.1.1(a));
voluntary, unlocks nothing, and the app is free.

6. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL AND WATCH PARTIES
A member may play their own audio file in; any member can delete any
recording. A watch party is behind Labs and carries no video of ours: this app
never fetches, decodes, stores or republishes a frame, and what travels through
it is a position and a clock. The video itself is played by YouTube's own
IFrame player, embedded unmodified, unobscured and with its own controls, on
whichever of the member's own signed-in devices they choose. Nothing is
downloaded, nothing is separated from its audio, and a channel with a party
loaded refuses to record.

GUIDELINE 1.2: no discovery and no directory - nothing can be browsed and
nobody found. A username names an invite link, not a search term, and a link
is minted by its owner and seats one person. The field taking an email
address does not search: it
sends a request there and returns nothing, not even whether an account
exists. Contacts need both sides to accept, only a contact can be
added to a channel, an address shows on a profile only if that person chose
to, and Ping is for contacts only.

New here, and the one place strangers meet: somebody who signs up without an
invitation and knows nobody is put, once, into a channel called "Getting
Started Cohort" with up to three others who joined around then, and one of
us. Nobody searched for anybody, nobody becomes a contact, only display
names show, and leaving is a tap. This demo account is not placed in one.

Guest links are how someone without an account takes part: a visitor knocks
under a typed name and a member admits them. Any member can withdraw a
guest's microphone or remove them, revoking their link; every guest goes when
the last member steps out. Recording happens only by a tap or the channel's
setting, and its indicator shows to guests too.
