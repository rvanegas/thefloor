3. WHAT IT DOES AND WHO FOR
A voice app for talking with people you already know. A call demands to be
answered now; a group chat never finishes a thought. A channel is a place rather
than a call — up to six people, keeping its name and recordings between
conversations, still there after everyone closes the app. Anyone present may
claim "the floor", and while they hold it every other microphone is withheld
for a minute: a way to be heard without being interrupted. Recording captures
each person separately. For small groups who already know each other. Not a
social network.

New in this version, and all of it inside a channel somebody already belongs to:
a shared clipboard holding one piece of text; guest links, described under
GUIDELINE 1.2 below; and per-channel notification settings, so one conversation
can be allowed to interrupt you and another not.

Open "Weekly Convo" > Step in (microphone prompt). Record/Stop; nothing asks for

Settings also holds Notifications, which is how loudly that one channel may

of its own, reached from the button at the top of Home, where a contact is added
by searching an email address. The Settings button beside it holds Appearance,
Privacy policy, Sign out and Delete account.

THREE THINGS THAT LOOK LIKE BUGS AND ARE NOT: "Claim the floor" is unavailable
while you are alone — claiming it asks a room to be quiet, and there is no room
to ask; a floor claim ends by itself after sixty seconds, which is the feature
rather than a timeout, and the countdown on the card says how long is left; and
a recording just stopped shows Play and Export greyed for a few seconds while
its mix is made.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
LiveKit (open-source, self-hosted by us) carries audio and produces recordings;
Amazon S3 stores them; Amazon SES sends sign-in codes; Apple Push Notification
service delivers notifications; Ko-fi hosts the donation page. No third-party
sign-in, payment processor, advertising, analytics or AI service, and no usage
data is collected. A guest joining by link speaks through the same self-hosted
LiveKit server, in the browser, with no account and nothing stored about them
beyond the name they type and the fact that they knocked.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
Identical everywhere but one: the Support screen's external Ko-fi link is shown
only in the US storefront, per 3.1.1(a). Voluntary, unlocks nothing; the app is
free everywhere with no in-app purchase.

7. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. All content is user-created. Two surfaces worth naming: a member
may play their own audio file into a channel, which a recording captures — their
file, their choice, in a private room; and a guest admitted by a member may
speak, and is recorded on the same terms as anybody else present, which the
recording indicator shows to everyone in the room including the guest.

GUIDELINE 1.2: no discovery, no directory, and no way to reach a person who has
not agreed to it. Nothing can be browsed and nobody can be found. The one field
that takes an email address does not search for it — it sends a request to that
address and returns no result, disclosing nothing about whether an account
exists. Contacts require acceptance on both sides, and an accepted contact is
the only person who can be added to a channel.

Guest links are the one way somebody without an account takes part, and they
are an invitation rather than a door left open. A member of a channel mints a
link and hands it out. Opening it does not join anything: the visitor types a
name and knocks, and somebody already in that channel has to admit them, one
knock at a time. A knock can be refused. A guest can have their microphone
withdrawn or be removed outright by any member present — not only by whoever
let them in, so a room is never stuck with a guest because one person stepped
out — and removing them revokes the link they came through. The link can also
be revoked from the channel's Settings at any time, which shuts it for everyone
holding it.

Two further limits are structural rather than settings anybody has to remember.
When the last member steps out of a channel, every guest in it is removed
automatically: nobody remains in a room with no member to answer for them. And
a link reaches exactly the channel it was made for and nothing else — there is
no way to walk from a channel to a person, to another channel, or to any list
of either.

Any member of a channel can delete any recording in it and may leave at any
time. Recording is never automatic and is visible to everyone present.
