# *Ask Claude*'s caps are 50, 150 and 450 words

Rodrigo's call, 2026-10-27, replacing the 30, 90 and 270 of
`2026-10-22-ask-claude-in-three-lengths.md` (20, 100 and 500 were tried
first the same day and turned down). Still a factor of three between *Medium*
and *Extended*, but *Brief* is no longer a single line. The shapes the prompt
pairs with each cap moved to match — two or three sentences, a short
paragraph, a few paragraphs.

*Medium* stays the default, so builds 386 and 387, which send no length, now
get 150 words where they were built against 100 — longer, not broken.

Standard's `max_tokens` went from 2048 to 3072: 450 words is about 600 tokens
before Haiku's low-effort thinking, and only generated tokens are billed. Pro
and Max were already at 4096 and 8192.
