1. SCREEN RECORDING AND DEVICES
None attached; section 3 walks the app in order. No purchases, no tracking, no
ATT prompt. Tested on iPhone 12 Pro Max through 16 Pro Max and on iPad, iOS
18.5 and 26.6. iPhone is portrait; iPad takes all four orientations.

2. WHAT IT DOES
Voice conversation between people who know each other. A channel holds up to
six and outlasts a conversation; anyone present may claim "the floor",
withholding every other microphone for a minute. New: iPad, one Home for
channels and contacts, single-use invite links, and a Labs setting, off by
default, hiding two unfinished features.

3. GETTING IN AND REACHING FEATURES
No passwords; a six-digit code is emailed. This account has a fixed
one: appreview@rvanegas.co / SIX-DIGIT CODE GOES HERE BEFORE SUBMITTING

No second person needed: the account holds a contact, Sam Rivera, and three
channels; "Weekly Convo" holds a recording, "Short Sample".

Home switches between Channels and Contacts. Open "Weekly Convo" > Step in
(microphone prompt). Mute, the floor and stepping out are in the bar at the
bottom.
Record/Stop - nothing asks for a name; Rename, Play, Export and Delete are on
the recording's row. "Play something together" plays a file from the phone and
"Shared clipboard" holds one text item; "Share a guest link" is in the
channel's Settings. Contacts holds requests, "Add a contact", and Copy Invite
Link once you have a username.

TO SEE THE EXPERIMENTAL FEATURES, turn on Settings > Labs > "Show experimental
features": Transcribe appears on the recording's row, and "Watch something
together" in the channel (section 6).

The same app runs at thefloor.rvanegas.co/app on this account; a channel holds
you on one device at a time.

NOT BUGS: "Claim the floor" is unavailable while alone; a just-stopped
recording greys Play and Export while its mix is made; a channel nobody speaks
in for fifteen minutes steps everyone out.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
LiveKit (open-source, self-hosted by us) for audio and recordings; S3 stores
them; SES for sign-in codes; APNs; Ko-fi for donations; AssemblyAI, sent
only a recording somebody asked to have transcribed, reachable only with Labs
on. No third-party sign-in, payment processor or advertising.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The Support screen's Ko-fi link appears only in the US storefront, per
3.1.1(a). Voluntary, unlocks nothing; the app is free everywhere.

6. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL AND WATCH PARTIES A member may play their own audio
file into a channel; any member can delete any recording. A watch party is
behind Labs and carries no video: this app never fetches, decodes, stores or
shows a frame, it shares a position and a clock. "Watch on another screen"
mints a link to a page where YouTube's own IFrame player plays it unmodified,
unobscured, with its own controls. Nothing is extracted, and a party running
refuses to record.

GUIDELINE 1.2: no discovery and no directory - nothing can be browsed and
nobody found. A username names an invite link and is not a search term. The
field taking an email address does not search either: it sends a request there
and returns nothing, disclosing not even whether an account exists. An invite
link is minted by its owner, seats the first person to open it, and discloses
that owner's display name to nobody else. Contacts otherwise need both sides to
accept, only a contact can be added to a channel, an address appears on a
profile only if that person chose to show it, and Ping is for contacts only.

Guest links are the one way someone without an account takes part: opening one
joins nothing, the visitor knocks under a typed name and a member admits them,
and may then ask them to become a contact. Any member can withdraw a guest's
microphone or remove them, revoking their link, and every guest goes when the
last member steps out. Recording is never automatic and its indicator
shows to everybody, guests included.
