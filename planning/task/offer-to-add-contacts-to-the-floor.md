# Offer To Add Contacts To The Floor

Offer the user their phone's address book, so they can bring the people in it
to The Floor. Each person either becomes a *contact* (GLOSSARY.md: somebody you
have both agreed to be in touch with) or, if they have no account, gets an
invitation. The matching is easiest once an account can carry a phone number,
which is `authenticate-with-phone-numbers-and-sms.md`.
