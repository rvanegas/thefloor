The Floor now runs in a web browser. Go to thefloor.rvanegas.co and sign in with the same account you use on your phone. You get your channels, the floor, and your recordings. Notifications still go to your phone; a browser tab can only show a dot on itself. You can only be in a channel on one device at a time, so opening one in the browser while you are already in it on your phone shows a Step In button rather than acting as though you are already there.

The channel screen has been rebuilt. Mute, the floor, and stepping in or out are now in a bar pinned to the bottom of the screen, so you can reach all three without scrolling. The channel name, Home and Settings are pinned to the top for the same reason, as they now are on Home and Contacts. The cards in between have been reordered: the floor comes first, directly under the list of people, because it decides which of them can be heard. If you would rather not have the controls appear twice, Settings has a new option, "Repeat the channel controls as cards", which removes the repeated cards and leaves the bar. The floor's card stays either way, because it is the only place that shows who is holding it and how long they have left.

Guests can now be added as contacts. If someone joins a channel through a guest link, any member of that channel can ask them to become a contact. The guest answers on the guest page, without leaving the conversation, and creates an account there if they do not have one. Each member's request is answered separately, so declining one does not decline the others. If you open a guest link for a channel you already belong to, you join as yourself instead of as a guest.

The Contacts screen and profiles have a new layout. Add contact is now the first row, and your own card is below it. Editing your name and bio is behind an Edit button on your profile rather than on a separate screen. A profile shows availability first, then the ways to contact the person: Ping appears at the top when you opened the profile from a channel they are not in, followed by their email address. Your own profile shows the email address you are signed in with, and you can now change it: enter a new address and confirm it with the code sent there. The channels you share with someone are listed on their profile, and tapping one now takes you there from anywhere, including from inside another channel.

The Home screen now counts other people. The time under each channel is when someone other than you was last in it, and channels are sorted by that. Channels someone else has visited sort above channels only you have opened. The mark showing that you stepped in and out now lasts fifteen minutes instead of five.

The floor is released when the person holding it disconnects. Previously the room stayed silenced for up to a minute waiting for a speaker who had already dropped. Disconnections are also detected faster: five to seven seconds instead of seven to seventeen. The roster shows when someone is still present but no longer sending audio, and you can now ping them while they are in that state instead of waiting for their connection to time out first.

Some settings now follow your account instead of the phone you set them on. Appearance, tap to step in, and the new channel controls option apply on every device you are signed in to, and change straight away on all of them. The Headphones option stays on the phone, because it is about the headphones you are wearing: Bluetooth headphones sound better when nobody is speaking and switch to a lower-quality connection when someone starts, which can clip the first word. Turn "Keep the connection steady" on and they stay on the speaking connection the whole time you are in a channel. Turn it off and the sound improves whenever the channel is quiet.
