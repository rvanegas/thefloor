# Update the introduction and send it to new users

planning/MANUAL.md is the introduction meant for somebody who has just joined,
and two things keep it from being that. **Nothing sends it**: no code and no
`bin/` script refers to it, and there is no on-joining email for it to be.
**And its central habit is out of date.** *The one habit* tells a person who
has pinged to stay reachable, because "a banner cannot fetch you from another
room"; since stepping in nearby, the listing says the opposite — ping, then put
the phone down, and when they arrive you hear them with the screen off. Rewrite
the habit section and anything else stepping in has moved, then send it once,
on joining, and otherwise only when a person asks — MANUAL.md § its opening
says why it must never be sent twice. MARKETING.md § *What marketing may not
do* governs the mail.
