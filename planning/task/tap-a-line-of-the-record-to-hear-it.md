# Tap a line of the Record to hear it

Rodrigo, 2026-11-16. Reading the *Record* and finding a line garbled, a
member taps it and hears that moment of the recording. Easy for a finished
segment, whose recording exists. **Not for the segment being recorded now**,
whose file does not exist yet; making it work would mean stopping the
recording and starting another so that the moment is in the past. Rodrigo's
call, to keep life simple: **a line in the segment currently recording cannot
be played**, and the member is told, or offered, that starting a new segment
makes it playable. *Better Controls for Playback* and *Improve Transcripts UI*
are bare titles that may have meant this; fold them in or delete them.
