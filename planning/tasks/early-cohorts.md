# Early Cohorts

To stimulate growth and familiarization with the app's functionality at this early stage, new users will be automatically introduced to a new channel with four new users and one of the app's root users, specifically Rodrigo or Rochelle. The channel is named "Getting Started Cohort <n>" with an incrementing <n>. It'll be possible to leave the channel, of course. Since users are not joining all at once, channels are created with exactly two members to start, until full, after which a new channel is created with an increment.

In the channel view, show a card below the tabs, explaining the purpose of this special introductory channel, including how to leave it.

Backfill to include the three cold installs already in the db in the first cohort, with Rochelle.

Change Privacy declaration accordingly. Note that this is not general discoverabililty or the introduction of social media or social networking. The intention is one cohort to introduce usability and features, after which contacts are entirely user driven. Indeed, though all are in the channel, none are contacts in advance.
