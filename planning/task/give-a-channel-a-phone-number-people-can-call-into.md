# Give a channel a phone number people can call into

From the call with Erta, 2026-10-02. The inbound half of
`dial-a-phone-number-into-a-channel.md`: a channel gets a phone number of its
own, and anybody who calls that number is in the channel. Rodrigo said he is
**less excited about this one** than the outbound call. Unlike outbound, the
caller is not someone a member chose, so that task's arguments about
*no strangers* and consent do not carry over and would need making again.
