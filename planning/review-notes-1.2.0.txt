3. WHAT IT DOES
Voice conversation between people who already know each other. A channel holds
up to six and persists between conversations; anyone present may claim "the
floor", withholding every other microphone for a minute. New here: a shared
clipboard, guest links (see GUIDELINE 1.2), a per-channel notification level.

4. GETTING IN AND REACHING FEATURES
No passwords; a six-digit code is normally emailed. This account has a fixed

You do not need a second person. The account holds one contact, Sam Rivera, and
three channels: "Weekly Convo" with her, holding a recording called "Short
Sample"; an older unnamed one; and "Just you".


Open "Weekly Convo" > Step in (microphone prompt). Record/Stop — nothing asks
for a name; Rename, Play, Export and Delete are on the recording's row. "Play
something together" plays a phone file into the room. "Shared clipboard" holds
one text item. "Share a guest link" is at the foot of that screen, and the
channel's Settings lists and revokes its links and holds Notifications — how
loudly this channel may interrupt you. "Start a channel" is the last row on
Home; Contacts and Settings are the buttons at its top, and Settings holds
Privacy policy, Sign out and Delete account.

NOT BUGS: "Claim the floor" is unavailable while alone, there being no room to
ask for quiet; a claim ends itself after sixty seconds, which is the feature; a
just-stopped recording greys Play and Export while its mix is made.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
LiveKit (open-source, self-hosted by us) for audio and recordings; S3 to store
them; Amazon SES for sign-in codes; APNs; Ko-fi for donations. No third-party sign-in,
payment processor, advertising, analytics or AI; no usage data collected. A guest speaks through that same self-hosted server, in the browser;
nothing is stored beyond the name they type.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The Support screen's Ko-fi link appears only in the US storefront, per 3.1.1(a).
Voluntary, unlocks nothing; the app is free everywhere.

7. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
All content is user-created; a member may play their own audio file into a
channel.

GUIDELINE 1.2: no discovery, no directory. Nothing can be browsed and nobody
found. The one field taking an email address does not search — it sends
a request there and returns no result, disclosing nothing about whether an
account exists. Contacts require acceptance on both sides, and only an accepted
contact can be added to a channel.

Guest links are the one way somebody without an account takes part, and they
are an invitation rather than an open door. Opening one joins nothing: the
visitor types a name and knocks, and a member must admit them, one knock at a
time; a knock can be refused. Any member present — not only whoever admitted them — can
withdraw a guest's microphone or remove them, and removal revokes that link; a
link can also be revoked from Settings, shutting it for everyone holding it.
When the last member steps out, every guest goes with them. A link reaches only
the channel it was made for, with no way to walk from it to a person or another
channel. A guest is recorded on the same terms as anyone present, which the
recording indicator shows to all, guest included.

Any member can delete any recording in their channel and may leave at any time.
