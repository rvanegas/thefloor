Claude's replies can be read by Pro voices: said once into the room, so everybody present hears the same reading, and kept in a recording. Read aloud on Channel Settings chooses Standard or Pro voices, and Floor Settings chooses your Standard voice and Pro voice for each language.

Read again, on the reading bar and on Claude's reply in the Record, says the reply into the room once more for everybody.

Name voices, in a segment's menu, gives each separated voice a name in place of its letter.

Play, in a segment's menu, opens Listen and plays it there.

Open noesis signs your browser in on the way. Send to noesis is gone. Switching your own library on in a channel is part of Pro; reading a library stays everybody's.

Your display name is the same in The Floor, noesis and Roxana. Deleting your account at auth.rvanegas.co deletes it here too.

Deleted channels and recordings are kept thirty days before they are gone.

A channel's place on Home is the same for every member.
