1. SCREEN RECORDING AND DEVICES
None attached - section 3 walks the app in order. No purchases, no tracking, no
ATT prompt. Tested on iPhone 12 Pro Max through 16 Pro Max, iOS 18.5 and 26.6,
portrait only.

2. WHAT IT DOES
Voice conversation between people who already know each other. A channel holds
up to six and persists between conversations; anyone present may claim "the
floor", withholding every other microphone for a minute. New: a browser version
of the same app, a rebuilt channel screen, guests who can be asked to become
contacts, and changing the address you sign in with.

3. GETTING IN AND REACHING FEATURES
No passwords; a six-digit code is emailed. This account has a fixed
one: appreview@rvanegas.co / SIX-DIGIT CODE GOES HERE BEFORE SUBMITTING

You do not need a second person: the account holds a contact, Sam Rivera, and
three channels; "Weekly Convo" holds a recording, "Short Sample".

Open "Weekly Convo" > Step in (microphone prompt). Mute, the floor and stepping
out are in the bar pinned to the bottom; the channel name, Home and Settings
are pinned to the top. Record/Stop - nothing asks for a name; Rename, Play,
Export, Transcribe and Delete are on the recording's row. "Play something
together" plays a file from the phone into the room, "Watch something together"
takes a YouTube link (section 6), "Shared clipboard" holds one text item, and
"Share a guest link" is at the foot of that screen. On Home, Contacts and
Settings are the top buttons; Settings holds Appearance, Headphones, Privacy
policy and Delete account.

The same app runs in a browser at thefloor.rvanegas.co/app, on the same
account and channels; being in a channel in one steps you out of the other.

NOT BUGS: "Claim the floor" is unavailable while alone; a just-stopped
recording greys Play and Export while its mix is made.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
LiveKit (open-source, self-hosted by us) for audio and recordings; S3 to store
them; Amazon SES for sign-in codes; APNs; Ko-fi for donations; AssemblyAI, sent
only a recording somebody asked to have transcribed. No third-party sign-in,
payment processor or advertising. Transcribing is never automatic and is free
once per account.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The Support screen's Ko-fi link appears only in the US storefront, per
3.1.1(a). Voluntary, unlocks nothing; the app is free everywhere.

6. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL AND WATCH PARTIES A member may play their own audio
file into a channel, and any member can delete any recording. A watch party
carries no video: this app never fetches, decodes, stores or shows a frame, it
shares a position and a clock. The phone is the remote; "Watch on another
screen" mints a link to a web page where YouTube's own IFrame player plays it
unmodified, unobscured and with its own controls. Nothing is extracted, and a
party running refuses to record.

GUIDELINE 1.2: no discovery and no directory - nothing can be browsed and
nobody found. The one field taking an email address does not search: it sends a
request there and returns nothing, disclosing not even whether an account
exists; changing your own address works the same way, confirmed by a code sent
to it. Contacts require acceptance on both sides, only an accepted contact can
be added to a channel, an address appears on a profile only if that person
chose to show it, and Ping is offered for contacts only.

Guest links are the one way someone without an account takes part: opening one
joins nothing, the visitor types a name and knocks, and a member must admit
them. A member may ask an admitted guest to become a contact, answered on the
guest page, each member's request separately. Any member present can withdraw a
guest's microphone or remove them, revoking their link; links are also revoked
from Settings, and every guest goes when the last member steps out. Recording
is never automatic and its indicator shows to all, guests included.

PLEASE TEST ACCOUNT DELETION LAST - it is immediate and leaves a working but
empty app.
