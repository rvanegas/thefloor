The Floor is in the browser now. Sign in at thefloor.rvanegas.co and you get the same channels, the same floor, the same recordings, on whatever you are already sitting in front of. The phone is still the one that reaches you when you are not looking — a tab can only put a mark on itself — so this is the second screen rather than the replacement. Your voice stays in one place at a time: opening a channel you are already standing in somewhere else offers to step in here rather than pretending you already have.

A guest can be kept. Anybody in the room can ask the person who came in on a link to become a contact, and they answer without leaving the conversation — they sign up where they are standing, in the room, rather than in an inbox. Each member gets their own answer, and saying no to one is not saying no to the room. If you follow a guest link to a channel you are already a member of, you arrive as yourself.

Contacts starts with adding somebody and then shows you your own card, and your profile is where your profile was always meant to be rather than behind an editor. A profile now leads with where somebody is, then with the ways to reach them: ping first when you opened it from a room they are missing from, then their address. Your own card shows the address you are signed in as, which the app had never actually told you.

Home is about other people. The line under each channel counts who else has been in it, not your own footsteps down the list, and orders the rooms by that. A room somebody else visited sorts above one only you have opened, and the mark that says you stepped in and out lasts as long as the rest of the room is still being told you are nearby.

Nobody holds the floor after they have gone. A speaker who drops out releases it the moment the room notices, instead of leaving everybody silenced for a minute waiting on a turn nobody is taking — and it notices sooner: five to seven seconds where it used to take up to seventeen. The roster says when somebody is present but no longer reaching you, before the connection has finished failing.

Bluetooth headphones have a setting under Headphones. They sound better when nobody is talking and have to switch when somebody starts, which can cost the first word. Keep the connection steady and they stay on the talking connection for as long as you are in a channel: quieter throughout, and nothing switches underneath anybody.
